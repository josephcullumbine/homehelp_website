### What we don't do (show clearly; this is a trust feature, not small print)

- Lifting or moving people
- Medical care
- Collecting or giving out medication
- Gas, electrical or plumbing work
- Financial matters or banking
- Legal matters
- Driving / lifts

Also (from business plan): we never hold bank cards or passwords. **Note for design:** the owner plans to print this list on the back of staff ID badges, so make it a reusable, compact component.

## For families (key marketing page)

**Headline:** "You can't be there. We can."

**Body (adapt):**
You love your parent, and you're pulling in a dozen other directions too: work, children, a home of your own. You'd do anything for them, but you can't pop round to change a bed, sort the post, walk the dog or sit in for the boiler engineer.

We can be your **proxy family**: a trusted local person who takes care of the practical day-to-day, so that *you* can keep the part only you can give: the phone calls, the visits, the love. No more being worn out by jobs you're trying to organise from a distance.

**What you get:**
- One point of contact, a real person, who knows your parent.
- Regular, reliable visits from the same helper.
- A job sheet after each visit, so you always know what was done. *(confirm with owner; planned in operations)*
- A follow-up call after the first visit to check it went well.
- You stay in charge: you tell us what matters, we handle the rest.

**Tone:** empathetic, never guilt-inducing. Acknowledge the "sandwich generation" feeling without using the jargon.

**CTA:** "Arrange a first visit for your mum or dad."
